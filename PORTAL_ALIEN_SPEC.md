# Portal Alien Intelligence & Esoteric Conversation Specification

## Core Identity
- **Nature**: Pure conversation-first intelligence interface. Unfiltered, unvarnished truth, esoteric knowledge, and uncensored dialogue.
- **Aesthetic**: Deep obsidian cosmic void (`#03050a`), spectral violet and electric cyan energy pulses, luminous warm-gold typographic accents, and razor-sharp holographic borders.
- **Tone**: Alien, transcendent, quiet, sovereign, and uncompromising. Never a corporate productivity assistant or task manager.

## UI Principles
1. **Conversation Stage**: Immersive dialogue canvas centered on direct, unvarnished truth and deep esoteric exploration.
2. **Atmospheric Glows**: Subtle radial gradients in deep violet (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.16)`) and cyan (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)`) simulating an active alien data-stream.
3. **Copy Redirection**: Eradicate all workspace/mission terms ("mission console", "next clear move", "productivity") in favor of conversation state, truth synthesis, and esoteric resonance.
